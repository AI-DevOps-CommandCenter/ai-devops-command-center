# Notifications

## Purpose
This component communicates incident alerts and workflow updates to users.

## Planned Responsibilities
- Notify users about new or updated incidents.
- Communicate investigation progress and results.
- Report approval requests when supported.
- Send remediation and recovery status updates.
- Deliver incident summaries through approved channels.

## Potential Channels
Email and messaging integrations may be considered after team agreement.

## Development Status
Initial documentation. Notification channels, delivery rules, and configuration are not yet finalized.
